Our story

A boutique hotel with a Georgian heart

Taberne opened in the spring of 2020 in one of the liveliest parts of Tbilisi — a short walk from the city’s restaurants, bars and main sights. Behind a red-brick façade are 43 rooms built around one idea: elegance and comfort, without the fuss.

How it started

Taberne is a young hotel with a simple ambition: to bring a fresh concept of accommodation to Tbilisi. It opened in the spring of 2020 on Vakhtang Orbeliani Street, in a neighbourhood full of restaurants, local bars and landmarks you can reach on foot.

The building holds 43 rooms for business and leisure travellers alike, each with free Wi-Fi and a calm, contemporary feel. The aim is the same in every one — comfort and quiet in the middle of a busy city.
